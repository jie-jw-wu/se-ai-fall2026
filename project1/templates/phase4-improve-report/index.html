    Phase 4 · Improvement Report (Route B)¶

    Name: · Date: · Target category:

    Two to three pages, plus the changed pipeline as working code in your repo. Use this template if you chose Route B — Improve. (Route A has its own QA report template.)

    1. The target¶

    Which failure category from your findings table, and how many rows of it you have. Say why you picked it — usually "the biggest pile", occasionally "the most dangerous".

    2. The hypothesis, written before the run¶

    "Most syntax failures come from truncated files in the corpus; removing files that do not parse will cut the rate."

    One sentence about the cause, not the symptom. If you cannot name a cause, you are about to make a change and hope — say that honestly instead.

    3. The change¶

    Exactly what you changed, at the level of detail someone could reproduce: config diff, corpus filter, fine-tuning set size, decoding settings. One change. If you made more than one, say so and accept that the attribution is weaker.

    4. Before and after¶

    Re-run the same Phase 3 probes on the new model: same prompts, same decoding settings, same eight categories.

    Category Before (rate) After (rate) Δ
    your target
    other categories…

    State the denominator (how many probes) and your decoding settings. If you changed the sampling temperature as your intervention, say how you kept the comparison fair.

    5. What it cost, and what it broke¶

    • GPU-hours spent on the retrain or the extra decoding
    • Validation loss before and after
    • Any category that got worse — report it plainly
    • Anything qualitatively worse (shorter outputs, more repetition, blander code)

    A change that fixes one category by degrading the model everywhere is a real finding. Hiding it is the only wrong answer.

    6. Did it work — and did it work for the reason you thought?¶

    Answer both. A rate that fell for a different reason than your hypothesis is common, interesting, and worth a paragraph. If the change did nothing, say so: a negative result reported with evidence scores exactly the same as a positive one.

    7. What you would try next¶

    One paragraph, with a week and a GPU you do not have.

    Demo video (3 minutes): show one probe before and after, and your before/after numbers.
