    AI-Use Log — Project 1¶

    Required. One row per meaningful interaction; a session of small completions can be one row. Aim for honesty and specificity over volume.

    Date Tool What you asked for What you accepted What you had to fix Verified how

    The rows that matter most¶

    Add these explicitly — they are the ones the reflection needs and the ones people skip:

    • It was confidently wrong. What did the assistant assert that was false? Invented API, wrong idiom, plausible-but-broken logic. How did you catch it?
    • It saved you real time. What would have taken an hour and took ten minutes?
    • You accepted something you did not fully understand. Everyone does this. Which was it, and what happened later?
    • You were faster without it. If that happened, say where.

    One-page reflection¶

    Submitted with the log. Address, in your own words:

    1. Where did AI assistance genuinely help in this project, and where did it not?
    2. Give one concrete case of it being confidently wrong. What was the failure, and what caught it — a test, a type error, a review, or luck?
    3. Did you understand your own pipeline less well in the places you leaned on it hardest? Be honest; the answer is often yes and that is worth knowing.
    4. What will you do differently in Project 2?

    Length is not the point. Four honest paragraphs beat two vague pages.

    Your log and reflection may be used in the class paper only with your consent, and consent has no effect on your grade. See ../../research/seet-paper.md.
